# Success Metrics
Targets are intentionally unset until a pilot establishes baseline data. Track:
- Organizer activation: first tournament published within 7 days of signup.
- Tournament completion rate: completed / started tournaments.
- Registration conversion: approved registrations / registration starts.
- Organizer repeat rate: organizer creates another event within 90 days.
- Result approval latency: median submission-to-approval time.
- Result correction rate: corrected approved results / approved results.
- Gamer profile completion and active gamer rate.
- Verified achievement coverage: eligible participants with generated verified record / eligible participants.
- Organizer portfolio coverage: completed EVOQ events represented in portfolio.
- Dispute incidence and platform reliability.

Metrics must derive from persisted event timestamps and approved results. Define denominators before dashboard implementation. Do not publish opaque reputation rankings.
