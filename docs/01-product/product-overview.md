# Product Overview
EVOQ is an esports professional ecosystem combining tournament operations with professional networking for gamers, teams and tournament organizers. Its differentiator is the connection between event outcomes and durable professional identity: approved tournament outcomes can create verified gamer achievements and organizer event records.

**Vision:** every gamer can establish competitive identity, every organizer can establish professional credibility, and every tournament can contribute to a verifiable esports career record.  
**Positioning:** The Professional Network & Operating System for Esports.  
**Tagline:** Play. Compete. Achieve. Get Recognized.

## Product areas
- Tournament Operations: create/manage events, teams, registration, rounds, groups, matches, scoring, results, standings, qualification, announcements and basic analytics.
- Gamer Network: public identity, IGN/game IDs, games/roles, achievements, proof, tournament history, basic statistics and team history.
- Organizer Network: individual and organization profiles, event portfolio/history, participant statistics, awards and verified EVOQ records.
- Data & Trust: provenance, result audit, achievement verification, organization verification, permissions, feedback and moderation.

## Target users
Initial focus: college esports clubs, independent organizers, grassroots organizations and competitive gamers. Adjacent audiences: gaming communities, event companies, teams/managers, sponsors and partners.

## Product principles
Tournament operations generate reliable data; profiles should connect to real activity; self-reported and verified records must remain distinct; paid status is not proof of trust; tournament formats must be flexible; V1 establishes the foundation and V2 expands the network/business ecosystem.
